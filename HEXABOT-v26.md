# HexaBot v26 — Autonomous Page Guide

HexaBot now behaves as an autonomous page companion on desktop:

- Moves to safe positions around the viewport by itself.
- Reacts to visible sections (services, methodology, free assessment and contact).
- Approaches the FREE ASSESSMENT section and actively recommends it.
- During the diagnostic invitation, clicking HexaBot starts the existing GAP assessment modal.
- Uses a pointing gesture and contextual speech bubble for the assessment CTA.
- Pauses roaming while the chat is open or while HexaBot is processing a message.
- Preserves a compact, non-roaming behavior on mobile and honors reduced-motion preferences.
- Eye tracking also works while HexaBot is roaming on desktop.
